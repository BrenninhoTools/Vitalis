Clinical Insights

The science behind recovery

Three principles that shape how the series approaches viral illness.

Hydration Science

Electrolyte balances maintain cellular fluid pressure during febrile responses. Oral rehydration solutions enhance systemic recovery significantly.

Fever Regulation

Fever is an evolutionary defense mechanism inhibiting viral replication. Antipyretics should be used strategically under clinical oversight.

Antiviral Timelines

Targeted antiviral treatments demonstrate maximum efficacy when administered within 48 hours of initial symptomatic onset.
